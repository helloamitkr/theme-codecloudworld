Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 29, 2026 — Effective immediately for all users.

Summary: We collect minimal data, never sell your personal information, and give you full control over what we store. This page explains everything in plain language.

1. Introduction

codecloudworld ("we," "our," or "us") operates the website codecloudworld.com (the "Site"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information about you when you visit our Site or sign up for our newsletter.

By using our Site, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use our Site.

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly

  • Email address — when you subscribe to our newsletter or contact us via our contact form.
  • Name — optional, provided when you fill out the contact form.
  • Message content — when you send us a message through the contact form.

Information collected automatically

  • Log data — IP address, browser type, pages visited, time and date of visit, and referring URLs.
  • Cookies — small files stored on your device to improve site functionality and measure traffic. See Section 6 for details.
  • Analytics data — aggregated, anonymized usage statistics via Google Analytics (see Section 5).

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To send you our weekly newsletter (only if you subscribed).
  • To respond to your messages and support requests.
  • To understand how our content is being used and improve the Site.
  • To detect and prevent fraud or abuse.
  • To comply with applicable laws and regulations.

We do not use your information for automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects.

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may share data with:

  • Email service providers (e.g., Mailchimp) to send our newsletter. They are bound by their own privacy policies and data processing agreements.
  • Analytics providers (Google Analytics) for anonymized site statistics.
  • Advertising partners — anonymized, aggregated audience data only. No personally identifiable information is shared.
  • Law enforcement — when required by law, court order, or to protect the rights and safety of our users or the public.

5. Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to collect anonymized data about how visitors interact with our Site. This includes pages viewed, time spent, traffic source, and general geographic location (city/country level only).

Google Analytics uses cookies to track interactions. You can opt out by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

We have enabled IP anonymization so that your full IP address is never stored by Google Analytics.

6. Cookies

We use the following types of cookies:

  • Essential cookies — necessary for the Site to function (e.g., session management). Cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics cookies — Google Analytics tracking cookies (can be disabled via opt-out link above).
  • Preference cookies — remember your choices (e.g., dark/light mode, dismissed banners).

You can control cookie preferences in your browser settings. Note that disabling certain cookies may affect Site functionality.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction — request that we correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion — request that we delete your personal data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Unsubscribe — opt out of our newsletter at any time using the unsubscribe link in every email.
  • Portability — request your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Objection — object to certain processing of your data.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at privacy@codecloudworld.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These include HTTPS encryption, access controls, and regular security reviews.

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security but are committed to protecting your data to the best of our ability.

9. Data Retention

We retain your data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy:

  • Newsletter subscribers — until you unsubscribe. We delete your email within 30 days of unsubscription.
  • Contact form submissions — up to 12 months from the date of submission.
  • Analytics data — 14 months (Google Analytics default retention).
  • Server log data — up to 90 days.

10. Children's Privacy

Our Site is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child, please contact us immediately at privacy@codecloudworld.com.

11. Third-Party Links

Our Site may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to review their privacy policies. The presence of a link does not imply endorsement.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify newsletter subscribers. Continued use of the Site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please reach out:
