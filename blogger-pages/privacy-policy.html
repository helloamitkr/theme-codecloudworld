🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 29, 2026 — Effective immediately for all users.

Summary: We collect minimal data, never sell your personal information, and give you full control over what we store. This page explains everything in plain language.

1. Introduction

codecloudworld ("we," "our," or "us") operates this website. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information about you when you visit our site or sign up for our newsletter.

By using our site, you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use our site.

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly

  • Email address — when you subscribe to our newsletter or contact us.
  • Name — optional, provided in the contact form.
  • Message content — when you send us a message.

Information collected automatically

  • Log data — IP address, browser type, pages visited, time/date, referring URLs.
  • Cookies — small files stored on your device to improve functionality and measure traffic.
  • Analytics data — aggregated, anonymized usage statistics via Google Analytics.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To send you our weekly newsletter (only if you subscribed).
  • To respond to your messages and support requests.
  • To understand how our content is used and improve the site.
  • To detect and prevent fraud or abuse.
  • To comply with applicable laws and regulations.

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may share data with:

  • Email service providers (e.g., Mailchimp) to send our newsletter.
  • Analytics providers (Google Analytics) for anonymized statistics.
  • Advertising partners — anonymized, aggregated audience data only. No PII shared.
  • Law enforcement — only when required by law or to protect users' safety.

5. Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to collect anonymized data about how visitors interact with our site. We have enabled IP anonymization so your full IP address is never stored.

You can opt out by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

6. Cookies

  • Essential cookies — required for the site to function. Cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics cookies — Google Analytics tracking (can be disabled via opt-out link).
  • Preference cookies — remember your choices (e.g., dismissed banners).

You can manage cookie preferences in your browser settings.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of your personal data.
  • Correction — request that we correct inaccurate data.
  • Deletion — request that we delete your personal data.
  • Unsubscribe — opt out of our newsletter at any time (link in every email).
  • Portability — request your data in a machine-readable format.

To exercise these rights, email privacy@codecloudworld.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures including HTTPS encryption, access controls, and regular security reviews. No method of internet transmission is 100% secure — we cannot guarantee absolute security but are committed to protecting your data.

9. Data Retention

  • Newsletter subscribers — until you unsubscribe. Deleted within 30 days after.
  • Contact form submissions — up to 12 months.
  • Analytics data — 14 months (Google Analytics default).
  • Server log data — up to 90 days.

10. Children's Privacy

Our site is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe we have, please contact us at privacy@codecloudworld.com.

11. Third-Party Links

Our site may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices. Review their policies independently.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we update the "Last updated" date above and notify newsletter subscribers where appropriate.
